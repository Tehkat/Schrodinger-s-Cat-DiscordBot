## 1. What the Bot does

The Bot posts digests of recently published biology-related research articles from the Nature Portfolio of journals. Article titles, links and journal names are retrieved from the public Crossref API. The Bot offers these slash commands:

- `/subscribe` and `/unsubscribe` start or stop automatic daily and weekly digests in a channel.
- `/today`, `/week` and `/top` show recent articles on request.

The Bot also replies "Meow" to messages that begin with the word "meow".
